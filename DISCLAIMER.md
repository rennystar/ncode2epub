This tool never fetches novel pages, illustrations or PDFs. You open the novel page and press 「縦書きPDF」 yourself; the tool only reads the file you saved.

Its developer guideline allows non-commercial use by registered 小説家になろう members and forbids, among others, commercial use, redistributing the data and using it as AI training data.
